sidequests

little missions around the city — pick one and go

☀️ Sun-Kissed Explorations

🐱

The Indiranagar Metro Cat NPC Encounter

Indiranagar Metro Station (Entrance A near the stairs)

Track down the city's most famous, totally unbothered calico cat catching sunbeams right by the commuter rush. Bonus points if you secure a selfie without waking them up.

🚌

Blind BMTC Bus Roulette

Majestic Bus Terminus (KBS) Platform 2

Hop on the first bright blue bus you see, buy a ticket to the absolute final stop, and wander through a completely new neighborhood. Pure golden-hour chaotic energy.

✈️

Aviation & Anxiety at Sadahalli

Sadahalli Quarry Road (Near the KIAL boundary wall)

Park by the open quarry fields near the airport to watch massive international flights land right over your head. Bring a picnic blanket, watch the sunset, and get into deep car talks.

☕ Cozy & Creative Vibes

🧩

Speed Puzzling & Filter Coffee

Underline Center, Indiranagar or Paper & Pie

Race your friends to finish a jigsaw puzzle at a sunlit local cafe. High stakes, deeply therapeutic, and heavily caffeinated.

🎨

Blindfold Portrait Painting

Bistro Claytopia, Koramangala or Firefly Terrace, Jayanagar

Hit a weekend art pop-up and try painting your friend's face while completely blindfolded. It will look absolutely chaotic, which is exactly the point.

🌳

Golden Hour Lalbagh Speedrun

Lalbagh Botanical Garden (East Gate entrance)

Wake up early to catch the morning glow, touch grass, dodge the cheeky monkeys, and find the prettiest hidden lakeside spots before the afternoon heat rolls in.

🍧 Sweet & Lazy Quests

🍫

Midnight Corner House Runs

Corner House Ice Cream, Residency Road or Jayanagar

The ultimate local sidequest. Race the closing timer at 11:45 PM to secure a warm, gooey Death by Chocolate sundae before the shutters go down.

Blossom Book House Digging

Church Street (The older, multi-story branch)

Spend a slow afternoon getting lost in the dusty, sunlit aisles of this iconic bookstore. Find a hidden gem, then read it at a nearby cafe.
